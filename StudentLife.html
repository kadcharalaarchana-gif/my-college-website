STUDENT LIFE IN PRAGNYA DEGREE COLLEGE

Student life at Pragnya Women's Degree College is full of learning, fun and memorable experiences. Students get opportunities to participate in cultural events, sports, seminars, workshops and various extracurricular activities. The college encourages students to develop knowledge, creativity, confidence, leadership and communication skills. It provides a positive environment for education, friendship and personal growth.

1.Academic Flexibility

  • Student learn different subjects.
  • They improve their academic knowledge.
  • Various learning activities support their growth.

2. Skill Development

  • Workshops help students improve their technical skills.
  • Seminars provide knowledge about new topics and technologies.
  • Students participate in competitions and other learning activities.

3. Festivals and Events

  • Cultural festivals encourage students to show their talents.
  • Technical events help students share their ideas and knowledge.
  • Sports activities promote teamwork and fitness.
