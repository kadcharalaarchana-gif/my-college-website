Departments

COMPUTER SCIENCE

Computer science is the study of computation, information, and automation, focusing on the theory, design, and application of computer systems to solve problems, encompassing everything from mathematical algorithms and software development to artificial intelligence and hardware interactions. It's about understanding how computers work and using that knowledge creatively and logically to build new technologies and efficient solutions, involving programming, data structures, and designing systems that shape our digital world.

B.SC

B.SC is an undergraduate degree program designed for students who are interested in science, technology, and research. It provides a strong foundation in scientific concepts, practical knowledge, and analytical thinking. Students explore different subjects such as Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Botany, and Zoology. The course combines theoretical learning with practical laboratory sessions to improve students' understanding and technical skills. The B.Sc. program helps students develop problem-solving abilities, critical thinking, research skills, and scientific knowledge. It also provides opportunities for higher education and career paths in IT, research, education, healthcare, and other science-related fields.

B.COM

B.om (Bachelor of Commerce) department focuses on undergraduate studies in business, accounting, and finance, offering core subjects like Financial Accounting, Economics, Business Law, and Taxation, with specializations in areas such as Accounting & Finance, Marketing, or Banking. It's a 3-year program designed to build foundational knowledge for careers in finance, auditing, management, and other commerce-related fields, with options for General or Honours degrees.
